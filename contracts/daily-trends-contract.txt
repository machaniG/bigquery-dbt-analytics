Dashboard: Daily Sales Tracking
Owner: Finance
Main question: How are sales, returns, and net revenue trending by day and country?

Required filters:
- Date
- Country
- Transaction type (optional)

Required metrics:
- Gross sales
- Returns and cancellations
- Net revenue
- Net units moved

Required dimensions:
- transaction_date
- country

Daily sales-tracking mart

| Column                    | Why Finance needs it          |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| transaction_date          | Time trend                    |
| country                   | Geographic filter/breakdown   |
| gross_sales               | Top-line sales before returns |
| returns_and_cancellations | Size of reversals             |
| net_revenue               | Revenue kept after reversals  |
| net_units_moved           | Volume trend after reversals  |
